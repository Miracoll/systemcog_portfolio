Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

At SystemCog (“we”, “us”, or “our”), your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and products, how we use it, and the rights you have. By using our services you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to provide and improve our services:

  • Information you give us — such as your name, email, and phone number when you contact us or submit a form.
  • Usage data — how you interact with our site, including pages visited and features used.
  • Technical data — device, browser, and IP information collected automatically to keep our services secure and reliable.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your enquiries, deliver and maintain our products, improve the user experience, keep our platforms secure, and — where you have agreed — send you relevant updates. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended without them.

Data Sharing

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our platforms, and only to the extent needed to deliver our services. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. While no system is completely secure, we work continuously to safeguard the data you entrust to us.

Your Rights

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please get in touch with us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us and we’ll be happy to help.
