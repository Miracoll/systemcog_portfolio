Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know about working with SystemCog. Can’t find your answer here? Get in touch and we’ll help.

What does SystemCog do?

We design, build, and maintain real software, IoT, and embedded products. We don’t just prototype — we launch and support live platforms, including products like Schoolpadi, Exampadi, Kwikride and Tysbe that thousands of people rely on every day.

What kind of projects do you take on?

We work across education, mobility, marketplaces, and more — from web and mobile apps to connected systems. If it involves turning an idea into a reliable, scalable product, we can help. You can browse our projects to see the range.

How does your working process work?

We follow a clear, end-to-end path: discovery and strategy, design and prototyping, development and integration, testing, launch, and ongoing support. You can read the full breakdown on our Working Process page.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope and complexity. During discovery we agree on a realistic timeline with clear milestones, so you always know what to expect and when. Smaller builds can ship in weeks, while larger platforms are delivered in planned phases.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we price based on scope, timeline, and the outcomes you need. After an initial conversation we provide a clear proposal with no hidden costs. Reach out for a tailored quote.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Shipping is the beginning, not the end. We monitor, maintain, and improve your product over time — fixing issues, adding features, and helping it scale as your business grows.

How do we get started?

Simply get in touch. Tell us about your idea or challenge, and we’ll set up a discovery conversation to map out the goals, scope, and next steps.
